# Owner's Guide — Managing Your Website

This guide is for the owner of **Vardhman Jewellers**. You do **not** need to know
anything about coding, GitHub or websites to manage your site. Everything is edited
through a simple dashboard called **Pages CMS**, and every change you save appears
on the live website automatically within a few minutes.

---

## 1. How to log in

1. Open **https://app.pagescms.org** in your browser (phone or computer).
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and log in with the GitHub account that was set up
   for you during website installation.
3. The first time, GitHub will ask you to install the **Pages CMS** app on the
   website repository — click **Install / Authorize**.
4. Select the website repository. You will now see the editing dashboard with
   sections like *Business Information*,*Jewellery Products*
   and *Gallery Management* and *customer reviews*

> There is also a discreet **Owner Login** link at the very bottom of your website
> that takes you to the same page.

## 2. What happens when you save

Every time you press **Save / Commit** in Pages CMS:

1. Your change is stored in the website's GitHub repository.
2. GitHub automatically rebuilds the website (this takes about 1–3 minutes).
3. The live website then shows your updated content.

You can watch progress in the repository under the **Actions** tab, but you do not
need to — just wait a few minutes and refresh the website.



## 4. How to add a new jewellery product

1. Open **Jewellery Products** → click **New entry**.
2. Give the file a name (e.g. `peacock-gold-necklace`) and fill in:
   - **Product Name** and a unique **Product ID** (e.g. `VJ-GN-002`).
   - **Jewellery Category** — pick from the dropdown.
   - **Photographs** — upload one or more photos (first photo is the main image).
   - **Purity**, **Gross Weight**, **Net Gold Weight**.
   
   - **HUID** — enter the article's real HUID. If you don't have it, leave it
     empty; the website will show *"Hallmark details available at store."*
   - **Availability** — Available / Sold / Made to Order.
3. Save. The product appears on the website after the rebuild.



## 5. How to upload a new ring photograph (example)

1. Open **Jewellery Products** → open the ring's entry.
2. Under **Product Photographs**, click to upload your photo.
3. Drag the photographs to reorder them — the first one is the main photo.
4. Save.

## 6. How to edit making charges

1. Open **Jewellery Products** → open the product.

1. Open **Homepage Content**.
2. Under **Homepage Banner (Hero)**, replace the banner image(s), headline,
   subheading and button labels.
3. Edit **Homepage Promotional Text** for the slim strip below the banner.
4. You can also switch entire homepage sections on/off under
   **Homepage Section Visibility**.
5. Save.

## 8. How to update your business details

Open **Business Information** to change:

- Business name, tagline, logo, favicon
- Phone numbers, WhatsApp number, email
- Shop address and Google Maps link
- Instagram / Facebook
- Opening hours and weekly holidays
- About Us heading, full description and photographs
- Shop interior and exterior photographs
- Footer text

Everything updates across the whole website (header, footer, contact page,
product enquiry buttons) after one save.

## 9. How to manage the photo gallery

Open **Gallery Management**:

- **New entry** → create a new album with a name, description and photographs.
- Reorder photographs by dragging them.
- Turn **Published** off to hide an entire album without deleting it.
- Turn **Feature on Homepage** on to show the album on the homepage.
- Delete a photo by removing it from the list.

## 10. How to manage categories (albums like "Gold Rings")

Open **Jewellery Categories**:

- Create, rename or reorder categories.
- Turn **Visible on Website** off to hide a category from customers.
- Empty categories are automatically hidden.

## 11. Testimonials and customer reviews

Open **Customer Testimonials** to add, edit, reorder, publish or unpublish short
customer quotes shown on the homepage.

Open **Customer Reviews** to manage full reviews — customer name, star rating,
review title, review text, the product purchased, and the date. Published reviews
appear on the homepage (top three) and on the **Reviews** page, which also shows
your average rating. Turn **Published** off to hide a review without deleting it.

## 12. How to rename jewellery items and sections

- **Rename a product:** open **Jewellery Products** → open the product → change
  **Product Name** → save. The new name appears everywhere (category grids,
  homepage sections, search, WhatsApp enquiry messages).
- **Rename a category** (e.g. "Gold Rings" → "Designer Gold Rings"): open
  **Jewellery Categories** → open the category → change **Category Name** → save.
  The web address of the category stays the same, so no links break.
- **Rename a whole section** (e.g. "Gold Jewellery" in the menu): open
  **Business Information** → **Jewellery Section Names** → edit the menu name,
  page heading and description for that section → save. The change applies
  across the navigation menu, homepage and collection pages.

## 13. Good habits

- **Update gold rates daily** — customers trust the prices they see.
- **Never invent a HUID.** Leave it empty if the article has none.
- Use clear, well-lit photographs (square photos look best for products).
- Turn **Demo Mode** off (in *Business Information* and *Daily Gold Rates*) once
  all your real content is entered.
